Dr. Khalida B. Soki

Dr. Khalida B. Soki is a board-certified Consultant Internist and Transplant Nephrologist practicing at Kidney Care Consultants at The Nairobi Hospital. She completed her transplant fellowship in November 2025 and serves as Medical Director of Figocare Plus dialysis units across Kenya.

She is Deputy Secretary-General of the African Association of Nephrology and an executive member of Women in Nephrology Africa, and previously served as Treasurer of the Kenya Renal Association. Dr. Soki has contributed to national kidney transplantation guidelines and is committed to advancing kidney care, education, and patient outcomes across Africa.
