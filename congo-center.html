Dr. Nlandu Mayamba N. Yannick

Specialist in internal medicine and nephrology, Dr. Nlandu Mayamba serves as Head of the Hemodialysis Unit at Kinshasa University Hospital and as a consultant physician.

He is actively involved in dialysis care, nephrology training, and medical education, with expertise in hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, and critical care nephrology. His work is supported by international training and participation in global nephrology programs and conferences, contributing to the advancement of kidney care in the region.
