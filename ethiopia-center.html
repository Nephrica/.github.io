Yewondwossen Tadesse Mengistu, MD

Yewondwossen Tadesse Mengistu, MD is a Consultant Nephrologist and Associate Professor of Internal Medicine at Addis Ababa University. With over 25 years of experience, his work spans clinical care, medical education, and physician training, and he has mentored many nephrologists across Ethiopia and Africa.

He played a key role in establishing Ethiopia’s first maintenance hemodialysis unit in 2001, helping expand dialysis services nationwide. Dr. Mengistu has authored over 40 peer-reviewed publications, presented widely, and held leadership roles in major national and international nephrology organizations.
